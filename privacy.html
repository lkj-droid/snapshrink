Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

SnapShrink is a static website that converts, compresses and resizes images on your own device. There is no image upload feature, no account, and no server that receives your files.

The short version: your images are opened, re-encoded and saved by your browser's own JavaScript engine. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Nothing about an image — its pixels, file name, EXIF data or GPS location — is stored by this site.

1. How image processing works

When you add a file, the browser reads it into memory, draws it onto an HTML5 canvas, and exports a new file in the format and size you chose. The download you receive is created in the same tab. The ZIP archive offered by “Download all” is also assembled in JavaScript on your device.

You can confirm this yourself: open your browser's developer tools, switch to the Network panel, and convert an image. No request will carry your image data.

We do not set advertising or analytics cookies ourselves. See section 5 for what changes if advertising is enabled.

3. Server logs

The site is served as static files by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives standard request information (such as your IP address, the page requested and your browser's user agent) when your browser loads the page. That information is used to deliver and secure the site and is governed by GitHub's privacy statement. It never includes your images, because your images are never part of a request.

4. Metadata and EXIF

Re-encoding an image through canvas keeps only its pixels, so EXIF metadata — camera model, capture time, and any embedded GPS coordinates — is not carried into the output file. Orientation is applied to the pixels before saving so photos are not rotated. This is a deliberate privacy benefit; the trade-off is that camera settings are not preserved.

5. Advertising

If advertising is enabled on this site, a third-party network (for example Google AdSense) may set cookies or use device identifiers to select and measure ads, and may process your IP address for that purpose. Advertising scripts never receive your images. Where required by law, a consent prompt will appear before any non-essential cookie is set, and you can decline it. You can also block advertising scripts with any content blocker; the converter itself keeps working, because it has no dependency on them.

6. Children

The site is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because we do not collect personal information at all.

7. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and Korea's Personal Information Protection Act give you rights to access, correct, delete and object to the processing of your personal data. Because this site holds no personal data about you beyond the theme setting stored in your own browser, there is nothing for us to export or erase on our side — clearing your browser's site data removes the only stored value.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will change too. Material changes — for example, adding a feature that transmits data — will be described here and, where appropriate, announced on the site.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's issue tracker at github.com/lkj-droid/snapshrink.
